################################################################################

File: Ensure-NetworkReady.ps1
- Reads trusted Wi-Fi profiles from trusted-wifi-networks.txt
- Also checks ASOLARIA_TRUSTED_WIFI_PROFILES env var
- Sets profiles to auto-connect
- Tries connecting with netsh wlan connect
- Probes internet via: Get-NetConnectionProfile, ping 1.1.1.1, msftconnecttest.com
- Waits up to MaxWaitSeconds (default 120) with retries every 4s
- Returns structured result: ok, reason, connectedProfile, attempts
