That is the right way to integrate advanced graph learning into a live agentic control plane. My view is that Asolaria is worth attention not because it is finished, but because it reveals the shape of the next layer. The important opportunity is not another chatbot shell. It is the governed operating system around frontier models: local execution, durable workflow lanes, compact tool integration, auditable authority, and telemetry rich enough to support future model-assisted control.
