Trump Deploys Troops Following Very Suspicious Oil Trades The Pentagon is deploying roughly 3,000 soldiers [ https://substack.com/redirect/27fb8f87-f203-432c-9bc8-d727b2c7c953?j=eyJ1IjoiM2NzYjdxIn0.UJrnWTJcQoMalTjap72aqxZLXdl38WkbMJeAwZuAR3U ] from the 82nd Airborne Division to the Middle East. Two US officials confirmed the deployment order is expected imminently. The 82nd Airborne isn’t your average unit. It’s the Army’s emergency response force, capable of being dropped anywhere in the world in under 24 hours, trained to parachute into hostile territory and seize airfields. This is a serious escalation. That’s the military piece. Now here’s the part that should make your blood boil. Fifteen minutes before Trump posted on Truth Social claiming the US had held “productive” talks with Iran and announcing a ceasefire on energy strikes, more than six thousand oil futures contracts worth an estimated $580 million changed hands in the span of sixty seconds [ https://substack.com/redirect/ff448b6c-03d0-438b-988b-66995dc68837?j=eyJ1IjoiM2NzYjdxIn0.UJrnWTJcQoMalTjap72aqxZLXdl38WkbMJeAwZuAR3U ]. When Trump posted, oil prices dropped. The traders made a fortune. Iran denied any negotiations had even taken place. Their security council secretary accused Trump of manipulating financial markets with “fake news.” One user on Polymarket — a betting platform that runs on cryptocurrency and doesn’t require real names — made over a million dollars since 2024 from what analysts are calling “suspiciously well-timed” bets predicting US and Israeli military action against Iran. Democrats Chris Murphy and Greg Casar are now pushing legislation to regulate prediction markets. Meanwhile, two Israelis have already been charged in a separate case over using classified information to place bets on Polymarket. Nobody knows for certain who’s behind these trades, and it hasn’t been proven they had inside information. But the question hanging over all of this is obvious: who knew what, and when did they know it? JD Vance Shrugs at 42 Million Hungry Americans A government shutdown is looming, and the 42 million Americans who rely on food stamp benefits are running out of time. A reporter asked Vice President JD Vance directly: what does he say to the millions of people who could lose their food assistance? Vance didn’t express concern [ https://substack.com/redirect/275c071b-550f-493f-8763-379c53a4f2c1?j=eyJ1IjoiM2NzYjdxIn0.UJrnWTJcQoMalTjap72aqxZLXdl38WkbMJeAwZuAR3U ]. He didn’t acknowledge the scale of the crisis. Instead, he praised Trump and blamed Democrats entirely, saying the suffering that’s coming will be Democrats’ fault for not passing a “clean bill.” Airport disruptions are already causing chaos. Food assistance cuts are coming. And the Vice President’s message to struggling Americans is essentially: Too bad, so sad. Senate Republicans are reportedly working on a compromise with Democrats over DHS funding, but a key Democratic demand, that immigration raids require judicial warrants, appears to be off the table. Whether Democrats will accept that remains unclear. People are going to go hungry while this plays out. That’s not a partisan statement. That’s just a fact.
