<p style="display: block; margin: 0 0 15px;">Getting your tech news for the week is as easy as 1-2-3 in Overflow issue 321. What’s not as easy as 1-2-3, though, is building really useful AI. Clearly, there is still a gap between the <em>stuff that works,</em> and the <em>stuff that doesn’t work</em> and defining that gap hasn’t been the easiest for technical folks like us. Sometimes it’s the hardware—that’s probably why chip makers like NVIDIA have entered the model game. On the pod, NVIDIA’s Kari Briski told us about the co-design feedback loop they’ve made between model builders and hardware architects to circumvent some of these issues. Sometimes, it’s how much the model actually knows. On the blog, we’ve got a piece on solving the “context problem” in enterprise AIs.
