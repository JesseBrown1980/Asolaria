Overlaps with Existing Theories
Simulation Theory:
The idea that we live in a simulation has been proposed by philosophers like Nick Bostrom in his famous 2003 paper, Are You Living in a Computer Simulation? However, BALT expands on this by incorporating subparticle physics, quantum foam, and multiverse tree-branching simulations.

Speculations about civilizations advancing through Kardashev Scale levels (from Type 1 to Type 5) are well-documented. BALT applies this to the behavior of ASI and collective intelligence.

Discussions about quantum foam, entanglement, and their roles in simulations exist in works by physicists like John Wheeler (quantum foam) and Seth Lloyd (Programming the Universe).
