You may receive starter code or be asked to write code from scratch.

System Design Interview
In this interview, you’ll be given an open‑ended or ambiguous problem. Your task is to break it down into components (e.g., authentication, storage, payments) and propose an overall architecture.

High complexity

Clear personal ownership

Meaningful impact
