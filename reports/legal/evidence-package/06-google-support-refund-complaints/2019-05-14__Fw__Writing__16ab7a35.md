Some people prefer to eat at food stands or restaurants.Others prefer to prepare and eat food at home. Which one do you prefer?

Actually, I did my search about how farmers feed their cattle and that made me change my mind. In other words, here in this  country, cows are feed by artificial food and chemicals and that makes them gain weight easily. However, in Argentina, as many other parts of Latin America,cows are feed by grass and water exclusively and that produces  a big difference in taste, flavor, tenderness and nutricioness.Sometines, I can eat a little bit of , what people called, organic meat.

Secondly, having the opportunity to cook at home is priceless to me. For example, I can got to the local market every Sunday  to buy natural and fresh vegetables and fruits. After that, I take my time to make my food, adding natural  olive oil and be aware of all the ingredients I am adding to my portions. I am completely and absolutely sure about the cleanliness in my kitchen and the high quality of the ingredients. In contrast, if you eat at a food stand or restaurant, you are not sure how spotless the kitchen is.
Moreover, there was a famous case near Times Square, where the owner of a hot dogs stand was a arrested due to the fact that the added dog’s meat to the sausages.

Finally, for all the reasons I mentioned above, I would rather produce my aliments at home rather than buying food outside .Since, it is less expensive and I can control the higiene, components and standards.
