Mindful Molecules: Synthesizing New Hope in Psychedelic Therapies

The focus will be on the innovative use of synthetic biology to develop novel psychedelic compounds, aimed at addressing the growing worldwide prevalence of mental health disorders such as depression, anxiety, PTSD, and addiction. This research has begun to reveal the efficacy and safety of these compounds, positioning them as potential alternatives to conventional treatments. Experts in the field will discuss the latest advancements in synthesizing psychedelic compounds, their potential therapeutic applications, and the ethical and regulatory challenges involved.
