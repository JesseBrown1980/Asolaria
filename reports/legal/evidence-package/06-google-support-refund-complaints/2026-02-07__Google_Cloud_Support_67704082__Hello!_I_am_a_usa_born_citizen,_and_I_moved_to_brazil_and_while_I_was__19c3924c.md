Hello Daniel,

Greetings from Google Cloud Billing Support.

I hope you are doing well. My name is Angelica, and I’m reaching out to you regarding your case on our recent chat. I am following up on your recent query, as you got disconnected during our last conversation.

I can certainly understand why you’re feeling stressed right now. Navigating the logistics of a cross-continental life with two young children is difficult enough without a bank transition throwing a wrench into your business and personal accounts.

It sounds like you are caught in a frustrating loop where your bank's switch from Mastercard to Discover combined with regional payment restrictions is putting your Google services at risk. I want to help you stabilize this so you don't have to worry about your account being blocked.

While you can often add a card to your Google profile, certain card networks (like Discover) have different acceptance rules when processed in Brazil versus the USA. If your Google Billing account is set to a specific region, it may reject a card that it perceives as "out of region" or unsupported for that specific local gateway.

To prevent any service interruption, please try the following steps:

Check the Billing Account Region, Ensure your Google Cloud Billing account region matches the "Home" address of the Discover card. If the account is set to Brazil but the card is US-based, the transaction may be flagged.

Contact Capital One (The Issuer). Since the bank replaced your Mastercard without your input, they may have placed a "travel block" or "foreign transaction block" on the new Discover card. Ask them specifically to authorize charges from Google Cloud originating in both the USA and Brazil.

Add a Backup Payment Method. If the Discover card continues to fail in Brazil, I recommend adding your Brazilian bank card or a different US-based Visa/Mastercard as a "Backup" in your Google Payment Methods. This allows the system to attempt a different card automatically before blocking the account.

Verify your Identity. Sometimes, moving between countries triggers a security hold. Check the Google Pay Alerts bell icon to see if you need to upload a photo of your ID to "unlock" international payments.


Upon checking the account 01E920-56F4E2-2FE183, I can see that there are no charges on it. In addition, the billing account has no issues related to the payment method. You've mentioned from our previous chat that the charges are from Google one and Google AI pro.

Your inquiry relates to a sibling product Google One that isn’t associated with the Cloud Platform and isn't supported through this channel.

On a best effort basis, I recommend you to contact Google One Support by following the steps on their Support Center listed below [1] and/or to consult their documentation.
