Dear *JESSE :*

Great news—you’ve advanced to the next stage of the hiring process for
our position! 🎉
​​​​​Your background stood out, and we’d like to learn more about your
communication style and approach to complex challenges through a *one-way
video interview.*

You should have received a separate email from *WedgeHR* with the direct
link and instructions for completing and submitting your video. Please
check your inbox (and spam folder, just in case).
About the Video Interview

This is a one-way process where you will answer a series of 5–7 questions,
allowing approximately 2–5 minutes for each response.

This stage is critical for two reasons:

   1.

   *Assessing English Communication:* The client uses this to directly
   evaluate your *fluency, professional tone, and clarity in spoken English*,
   which is a key requirement for success in this role.
   2.

   *Evaluating Strategic Fit:* The questions are situational, focusing on
   your experiences, problem-solving approach, and career development pursuits.

5 Quick Tips for a Successful Recording

Here are a few quick tips to help you feel confident during the recording:

   1.

   *Be Natural and Authentic:* You'll have a *minimum* of 45 seconds to
   prepare your thoughts before recording. We highly encourage you to use
   notes or an outline, but please ensure your delivery is natural,
   conversational, and sounds confident, rather than reading directly from a
   script/AI-generated answer.
   2.

   *Optimize Your Environment:* Record in a quiet space free from
   background noises. If possible, use a high-quality noise-canceling headset
   to ensure the sound quality is clear. Test your audio before recording.
   3.

   *Check Your Setup:* We highly recommend using the laptop or PC you plan
   to use for daily work. This not only ensures high video and audio clarity
   but also helps us verify that you have a functional device ready for remote
   video calls.
   4.

   *Reduce Distractions:* Use a professional virtual background if you do
   not have a dedicated home office space. Ensure you have clear lighting so
   your face is easily visible.
   5.

   *Technical Issues:* If you encounter any technical difficulty with the
   WedgeHR platform (requires stable internet), please let us know immediately
   so we can assist you or reset your invitation.

We look forward to reviewing your submission and learning more about how
you can drive growth in this exciting role.
