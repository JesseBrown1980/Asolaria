<p class="paragraph" style='text-align: center; line-height: 1.4; color: #000000; font-family: "times new roman", times; font-size: 15px; margin: 10px 0;' align="center"><span class="normaltextrun" style="line-height: 1.4;"><strong>ICE Requests Utah Authorities to Not Release Criminal Illegal Alien Accused of Sexually Assaulting a 14-Year-Old Girl</strong></span></p>
<p class="paragraph" style='text-align: center; line-height: 1.4; color: #000000; font-family: "times new roman", times; font-size: 15px; margin: 10px 0;' align="center"><span class="normaltextrun" style="line-height: 1.4;"><em>This criminal’s immigration case was marked as a non-enforcement priority by the Biden administration in 2022</em></span></p>
<p style='line-height: 1.4; color: #000000; font-family: "times new roman", times; font-size: 15px; text-align: left; margin: 10px 0;' align="left">WASHINGTON – Today, the U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) announced a U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) detainer on 24-year-old Conrrado Ahuexoteco Atrisco, a criminal illegal alien from Mexico charged for <strong>sexually assaulting a 14-year-old Park City High School student.</strong></p>
<p style='line-height: 1.4; color: #000000; font-family: "times new roman", times; font-size: 15px; text-align: left; margin: 10px 0;' align="left">According to <a href="https://links-1.govdelivery.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fgcc02.safelinks.protection.outlook.com%2F%3Furl=https%253A%252F%252Fwww.abc4.com%252Fnews%252Fcrimes-against-children%252F24-yo-arrested-sexually-assaulting-high-school-student%252F%26data=05%257C02%257CERIC.LENDRUM%2540hq.dhs.gov%257C6cc9e0b889154d9132db08de7e116232%257C3ccde76c946d4a12bb7afc9d0842354a%257C0%257C0%257C639086810299196893%257CUnknown%257CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%253D%253D%257C0%257C%257C%257C%26sdata=6ZaKdYTUa6jZTmib7PBJ4%252BB9kzqKa6Jmbkd%252B1dEa724%253D%26reserved=0/1/0100019cd46a03de-e11ddd5f-04a9-4cc2-b37b-06f405fcf7eb-000000/u1xszw9H9yHe6BZAnVkPXStc6H_HHcdm7IT_FWK-D7s=447" target="_blank" rel="noopener" style="color: #00568C; text-decoration: underline; -ms-word-break: break-all; word-break: break-word; -webkit-hyphens: none; -moz-hyphens: none; hyphens: none;">local reporting</a>, the victim and Atrisco were at a friend’s apartment when he began forcibly touching and sexually assaulting her. The victim was watching TikTok videos when Conrrado Ahuexoteco Atrisco came over and asked if he could watch the videos with her.</p>
<p style='line-height: 1.4; color: #000000; font-family: "times new roman", times; font-size: 15px; text-align: left; margin: 10px 0;' align="left">She said yes, and he got on the bed with her. When he started hugging her and trying to cuddle, she asked him to stop, and she did not want to be touched. He pulled her sweatpants down and began to sexually assault her.</p>
<p style='line-height: 1.4; color: #000000; font-family: "times new roman", times; font-size: 15px; text-align: left; margin: 10px 0;' align="left">Atrisco and his mother previously entered the U.S. illegally near the Sonora desert and were arrested and removed by U.S. Border Patrol. Atrisco re-entered the country again, a felony, only to have the Biden administration mark his immigration case as a non-enforcement priority. Since his release, he went on to commit this heinous crime. ICE lodged an arrest detainer on March 5.</p>

Under President Trump, we will continue to fight for the arrest and removal of criminal illegal aliens to protect American families.”</em></span></p>
