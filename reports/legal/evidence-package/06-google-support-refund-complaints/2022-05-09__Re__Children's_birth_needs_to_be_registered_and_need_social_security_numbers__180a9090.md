Thank you for your message.  In order to request an appointment for a Consular Report of Birth Abroad (CRBA) for your child, please follow the instructions below.

  1.  Print the CRBA checklist attached to this email;

  1.  Gather all required documents, including the required application forms listed on the CRBA checklist;

  1.  Scan and send the following to saopaulocrba@state.gov<mailto:saopaulocrba@state.gov>. Files must me attached to the e-mail in PDF format. Please do not insert files on the body of the e-mail, for these will not be accepted:

  *   All documents and completed forms listed on the CRBA Check list
  *   Parent’s passport bio page or other official identification;
  *   A local (Brazil) phone number where you can be easily reached.

  1.  On the subject of the email, please put “CRBA Application Pack – Name of your child”;

  1.  All requests for CRBA appointments must be done via e-mail;

  1.  Please be advised that we will respond your e-mail with an appointment date and time within 5 business days after receiving your application. If you are not yet prepared to confirm your appointment, please DO NOT E-MAIL your packet.

  1.  CRBA appointments are in high demand and appointments can be rescheduled for emergencies only on a case by case basis. If your situation is not considered an emergency, you will need to proceed with the appointment given to you. The Consulate will only entertain requests for emergency rescheduling THREE (3) BUSINESS days prior to the appointment. Applicants who fail to show up for their scheduled appointments must start the appointment request process from the beginning.

  1.  Once your packet is received, our staff will e-mail you the date and time of your CRBA appointment and, if needed, a list of items you are missing.

  1.  Although it is not required, a personal appearance by the applicant (the child) is strongly recommended.  Please note that if you are applying for the child’s passport at the same time as the report of birth abroad, the child must be present.

  1.  Please arrive on time to your appointment. We have a grace period of 30 minutes for late arrivals. After this time, you will need to reschedule your appointment.  Your appointment time is not the same as your interview time.  Interview time depend on the number of applicants who appear for their appointments as well as the length of processing time for each case.

  1.  Please bring photos, originals of all required documents and I.Ds with you on the day of your appointment!  Please refer to the CRBA checklist for details on required items.

  1.  Please come prepared. An incomplete pack may result in delays in the CRBA application process.
