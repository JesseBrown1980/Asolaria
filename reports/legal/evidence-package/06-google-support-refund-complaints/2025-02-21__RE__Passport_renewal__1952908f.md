Hi, Jesse.
I examined your information in the system and can issue you a "TAMBADAL" travel document (a temporary certificate instead of a national passport). The reason for this is that you haven't been to Israel in a long time since making "Alyia".
The "Tambadal" is a travel document that is practically identical to a passport, with only the exception of the color of the cover. It provides free entry to Israel and allows for smooth passage through all flight connections.

However, If you want to go elsewhere than Israel, you must apply for a visa to access those countries. If this is the case, you will need to contact the Embassy of the nation you wish to visit in order to apply for a visa.

For issuing a Tambadal you will need to set an appointment in the embassy (The address is here below my signature)  and bring with you:
 1. Your old passport
2. Submit two updated 5*5 passport photos with a bright background, focusing on the face.
3. Two forms that  are attached to this email.

The cost of issuing a Tambadal is R$207.
Keep in mind that your Tambadal will expire in a maximum of one year.

Hope everything is clear.

Please let me know if you need anything else.
