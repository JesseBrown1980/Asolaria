# Forestier syndrome presenting with dysphagia: case report of a rare presentation

**From:** Jesse Daniel Brown <plasmatoid@gmail.com>

## Body (extracted text)

I have encountered a patient in Brazil with a very rare disease during my research relating to AI spinal surgery treatments, and I found a pre existing case (paper uploaded) that was treated (a lesser case) exhibiting Forestier syndrome (Diffuse Idiopathic Skeletal Hyperostosis, DISH) presenting with dysphagia. The new patient, Celso Campos, is extremely rare and shows opportunity for research and publication. This is because DISH is a relatively rare condition, and the presentation of dysphagia as a symptom is even rarer. As the paper example shows, we have a patient with 77 years old showing a lesser state of dysphagia who underwent treatment: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5760404/ However, the newer case of Mr. Campos, is more complex. I would like to speak with the specialist related to this field to organize a meeting and create a research and publication opportunity with the use of AI in the surgical removal of the ossified materials. Looking forward to a response.
