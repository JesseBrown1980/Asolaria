Hi Jesse ,
We do not have consular services in Rio. Israel's embassy is in Brasilia, and we also provide consular services in Sao Paolo.

I checked your details in the system and we can issue you a travel document called “TAMBADAL”.  The reason for this lies in the fact that you have not been in Israel for a long period of time since you made “Alyia”.

The “Tambadal” is a travel document practically identical to a passport, (the only changing is the color of the cover) and with this document you have free entry to Israel passing through all flight connections without problems. The Tambadal  we can issue for you can be valid for only 1 year and the fee for that is 207 Reais

However, if you want to travel to places other than Israel, you will need to apply for a visa to enter the other countries. If that is the case, you will have to contact  the embassy of the country you want to visit in order to apply for a visa.

In your case, depending on your domicile, you will need to appear in person in either Sao Paulo or Brasilia to obtain the travel document.
It is necessary that you make an appointment. You will need to bring with you your old passport and 2 recent passport photos 5 by 5 cm—(a face photo against a bright background) and the 2 document attached to this email.

Hope things are more clear now.

Let me know if you need anything else,

Nurit
