As I see that 2 days ago you posted on your Linkedin ( https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7157915813914521601/ ) "O Hospital Israelita Albert Einstein, em São Paulo, implementou um sistema de Inteligência Artificial (IA) para prever internações hospitalares. The new patient, Celso Campos, is extremely rare and shows opportunity for research and publication. This is because DISH is a relatively rare condition, and the presentation of dysphagia as a symptom is even rarer. As the paper example shows, we have a patient with 77 years old showing a lesser state of dysphagia who underwent treatment: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5760404/ However, the newer case of Mr. Campos, is more complex.
