1. Child of U.S. Citizen Father

General Requirements for Fathers of Children Born Out of Wedlock

The general requirements for acquisition of citizenship at birth[21]<https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-21> for a child born in wedlock also apply to a child born out of wedlock outside of the United States (or one of its outlying possessions) who claims citizenship through a U.S. citizen father.   *   The child’s father (unless deceased) has agreed in writing to provide financial support for the child until the child reaches 18 years of age; and

  *   One of the following criteria is met before the child reaches 18 years of age:

     *   The child is legitimated under the law of his or her residence or domicile;

     *   The father acknowledges in writing and under oath the paternity of the child; or

     *   The paternity of the child is established by adjudication of a competent court.

In addition, the residence or physical presence requirements contained in the relevant paragraph of INA 301<http://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1401&num=0&edition=prelim> continue to apply to children born out of wedlock, who are claiming citizenship through their fathers.

Written Agreement to Provide Financial Support

In order for a child born out of wedlock outside of the United States (or one of its outlying possessions) to acquire U.S. citizenship through his or her father, Congress included a requirement that the father agree in writing to provide financial support for the child until the child reaches the age of 18.[22]<https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-22> Congress included the language to prevent children from becoming public charges.[23]<https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-23> USCIS interprets the phrase in the statute “has agreed in writing to provide financial support”[24]<https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-24> to mean that there must be documentary evidence that supports a finding that the father accepted the legal obligation to support the child until the age of 18.

A written agreement of financial support is not required if the father died before the child’s 18th birthday.[26]<https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-26>

 the child until the child reaches the age of 18.<a href="https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-22" data-auth="NotApplicable" title="" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);"><sup style="box-sizing:border-box; font-size:12px; line-height:0; top:-0.5em"><strong style="box-sizing:border-box; font-weight:bold">[22]</strong></sup></a><span>&nbsp;</span>Congress
 included the language to prevent children from becoming public charges.<a href="https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-23" data-auth="NotApplicable" title="" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);"><sup style="box-sizing:border-box; font-size:12px; line-height:0; top:-0.5em"><strong style="box-sizing:border-box; font-weight:bold">[23]</strong></sup></a><span>&nbsp;</span>USCIS
 interprets the phrase in the statute “has agreed in writing to provide financial support”<a href="https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-24" data-auth="NotApplicable" title="" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);"><sup style="box-sizing:border-box; font-size:12px; line-height:0; top:-0.5em"><strong style="box-sizing:border-box; font-weight:bold">[24]</strong></sup></a><span>&nbsp;</span>to
 mean that there must be documentary evidence that supports a finding that the father accepted the legal obligation to support the child until the age of 18.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; color: rgb(68, 68, 68); font-family: source_sans_pro_regular, &quot;Source Sans Pro&quot;, Arial, sans-serif; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
The written agreement of financial support may be dated at any time before the child’s 18th birthday. If the child is under the age of 18 at the time of filing an Application for Certificate of Citizenship, the father may provide the written agreement of financial
 support either concurrently with the filing of the application or prior to the adjudication of the application. USCIS may request the written agreement of financial support at the time of issuance of a Request for Evidence or at the time of an interview (unless
 the interview is waived).</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; color: rgb(68, 68, 68); font-family: source_sans_pro_regular, &quot;Source Sans Pro&quot;, Arial, sans-serif; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
Alternatively, if the applicant is already over the age of 18, he or she may meet the requirement if one or more documents support a finding that the father accepted his legal obligation to support the child. In such cases, the evidence must have existed (and
 have been finalized) prior to the child’s 18th birthday and must have met any applicable foreign law or U.S. law governing the child’s or father’s residence to establish acceptance of financial responsibility.<a href="https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-25" data-auth="NotApplicable" title="" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);"><sup style="box-sizing:border-box; font-size:12px; line-height:0; top:-0.5em"><strong style="box-sizing:border-box; font-weight:bold">[25]</strong></sup></a></p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; color: rgb(68, 68, 68); font-family: source_sans_pro_regular, &quot;Source Sans Pro&quot;, Arial, sans-serif; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
In all cases, the applicant has the burden of proving the father has met any applicable requirements under the law to make an agreement to provide financial support. A written agreement of financial support is not required if the father died before the child’s
 18th birthday.<a href="https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-26" data-auth="NotApplicable" title="" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);"><sup style="box-sizing:border-box; font-size:12px; line-height:0; top:-0.5em"><strong style="box-sizing:border-box; font-weight:bold">[26]</strong></sup></a></p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; color: rgb(68, 68, 68); font-family: source_sans_pro_regular, &quot;Source Sans Pro&quot;, Arial, sans-serif; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
<em style="box-sizing:border-box">Written Agreement Requirements</em></p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; color: rgb(68, 68, 68); font-family: source_sans_pro_regular, &quot;Source Sans Pro&quot;, Arial, sans-serif; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
In order for a document to qualify as a written agreement of financial support under<span>&nbsp;</span><a href="http://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title8-section1409&amp;num=0&amp;edition=prelim" data-auth="NotApplicable" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);">INA
 309(a)(3)</a>, the document:</p>
<ul style="box-sizing: border-box; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; margin-left: 24px; padding-left: 8px; color: rgb(68, 68, 68); font-family: source_sans_pro_regular, &quot;Source Sans Pro&quot;, Arial, sans-serif; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
<li style="box-sizing:border-box; margin-bottom:8px">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;box-sizing:border-box; margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:16px">
Must be in writing and acknowledged by the father;<a href="https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-27" data-auth="NotApplicable" title="" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);"><sup style="box-sizing:border-box; font-size:12px; line-height:0; top:-0.5em"><strong style="box-sizing:border-box; font-weight:bold">[27]</strong></sup></a></p>
</li><li style="box-sizing:border-box; margin-bottom:8px">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;box-sizing:border-box; margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:16px">
Must indicate the father’s agreement to provide financial support for the child;<a href="https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-28" data-auth="NotApplicable" title="" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);"><sup style="box-sizing:border-box; font-size:12px; line-height:0; top:-0.5em"><strong style="box-sizing:border-box; font-weight:bold">[28]</strong></sup></a><span>&nbsp;</span>and</p>
</li><li style="box-sizing:border-box; margin-bottom:8px">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;box-sizing:border-box; margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:16px">
Must be dated before the child’s 18th birthday.</p>
</li></ul>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; color: rgb(68, 68, 68); font-family: source_sans_pro_regular, &quot;Source Sans Pro&quot;, Arial, sans-serif; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
In addition, USCIS considers whether the agreement was voluntary.</p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; color: rgb(68, 68, 68); font-family: source_sans_pro_regular, &quot;Source Sans Pro&quot;, Arial, sans-serif; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
<em style="box-sizing:border-box">Other Acceptable Documentation</em></p>
<p style="box-sizing: border-box; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; color: rgb(68, 68, 68); font-family: source_sans_pro_regular, &quot;Source Sans Pro&quot;, Arial, sans-serif; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
A written agreement of financial support may come in different forms and documents. USCIS may consider other similar documentation in which the father accepts financial responsibility of the child until the age of 18. Some examples of documents USCIS may consider
 include:</p>
<ul style="box-sizing: border-box; margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 16px; margin-left: 24px; padding-left: 8px; color: rgb(68, 68, 68); font-family: source_sans_pro_regular, &quot;Source Sans Pro&quot;, Arial, sans-serif; background-color: rgb(255, 255, 255);">
<li style="box-sizing:border-box; margin-bottom:8px">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;box-sizing:border-box; margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:16px">
A previously submitted Affidavit of Support (<a href="https://www.uscis.gov/i-134" data-auth="NotApplicable" data-entity-substitution="canonical" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="f4dac84e-f730-465e-8f4f-28cfb9dd9740" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);">Form
 I-134</a>) or Affidavit of Support Under Section 213A of the INA (<a href="https://www.uscis.gov/i-864" data-auth="NotApplicable" data-entity-substitution="canonical" data-entity-type="node" data-entity-uuid="224b9c85-1034-4a07-80d3-8347ca130377" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);">Form
 I-864</a>);</p>
</li><li style="box-sizing:border-box; margin-bottom:8px">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;box-sizing:border-box; margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:16px">
Military Defense Enrollment Eligibility Reporting System (DEERS) enrollment;</p>
</li><li style="box-sizing:border-box; margin-bottom:8px">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;box-sizing:border-box; margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:16px">
Written voluntary acknowledgement of a child in a jurisdiction where there is a legal requirement that the father provide financial support;<a href="https://www.uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-12-part-h-chapter-3#footnote-29" data-auth="NotApplicable" title="" style="box-sizing: border-box; text-decoration: underline; color: rgb(0, 102, 153);"><sup style="box-sizing:border-box; font-size:12px; line-height:0; top:-0.5em"><strong style="box-sizing:border-box; font-weight:bold">[29]</strong></sup></a></p>
</li><li style="box-sizing:border-box; margin-bottom:8px">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;box-sizing:border-box; margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:16px">
Documentation establishing paternity by a court or administrative agency with jurisdiction over the child’s personal status, if accompanied by evidence from the record of proceeding establishing the father initiated the paternity proceeding and the jurisdiction
 legally requires the father to provide financial support; or</p>
</li><li style="box-sizing:border-box; margin-bottom:8px">
<p style="margin-top: 0px; margin-bottom: 0px;box-sizing:border-box; margin-top:0px; margin-bottom:16px">
A petition by the father seeking child custody or visitation with the court of jurisdiction with an agreement to provide financial support and the jurisdiction legally requires the father to provide financial support.</p>
</li></ul>
