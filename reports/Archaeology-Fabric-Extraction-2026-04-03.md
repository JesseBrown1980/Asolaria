# Archaeology Fabric Extraction

Verdict: the archaeology is a gold mine for structure, not for story.

## Keep

- boot-critical startup ladders
- chain-first navigation
- typed catalogs
- reverse lookup
- duplicate merge/compression
- device-scope truth
- proof packet grammar
- PID lifecycle
- self-verification
- helper-bus backbone

## Demote

- mythology in the operational layer
- browser/search drift
- Bluetooth / SmartThings / Phone Link as fake routes
- screenshot forests
- redundant latest wrappers
- machine-specific scars that do not generalize

## Meaning

The new fabric does not need to be invented from nothing. Much of its grammar, registry logic, packet shapes, and proof discipline already exists in the archaeology. The work is extraction and recombination, not wholesale revival.
